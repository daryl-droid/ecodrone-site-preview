Seed pelleting & coating

The process and the benefits

  • Pelleted seed with a hydrophilic substrate has higher germination rates in difficult environments.
  • The pelleting process is customized and tailored to site-specific needs, using the client’s own seed.
  • Seed spreads much more evenly.
  • Lower emissions: this saves the need for track packing seed into place.
  • Seed weight increases about 4–6 times, depending on the seed. Many native species have tiny seed, so the pellets are less vulnerable to movement by wind and similar.
  • Fertilizers and mycorrhizal fungi can be added to the pellet.
