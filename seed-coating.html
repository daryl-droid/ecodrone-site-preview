Seed pelleting & coating

The process and the benefits

  • Pelletized seeds with hydrophilic substrate have increased germination rates in challenging environments.
  • The pelleting process is tailored to site-specific needs using the client’s seed.
  • Pelletized seed spreads evenly and accurately with less drift from wind.
  • Lowered cost by lessening the need for track packing into place. The seed has its own substrate and soil contact.
  • Seed weight increases up to 4–6 times, depending on the seed. Many native seeds are tiny. Pelletized seeds are less vulnerable to movement by wind.
  • Seeds can also be treated. Custom mixes using fertilizers and mycorrhizal fungi can be added to the coating to increase germination rates if the client requires.
