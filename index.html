01 / Who we are

Trusted experts in industrial drone operations

We combine commercial drone technology with decades of hands-on pipeline and construction work. We understand the pressure of staying compliant, on budget and on time, because we’ve lived it.

01

Seed & material dispersal

GPS-guided dispersal of seed, mulch and granular material, with no ground disturbance and no heavy equipment on the slope.

02

Heavy material lifting

We move seedlings, materials and gear by drone across rivers, steep or roadless terrain, and remote sites, reducing helicopter and ground-crew risk. Coming soon: major heavy-lift upgrades.

03

Silviculture & reforestation

We deliver tree seedlings by drone to hard-to-reach planting sites: steep slopes, across rivers, burned areas and places with no road access, cutting down on hand-carrying and helicopter time.

04

Land & pipeline measurement

Drone survey of trench volume and surface contours for planning, compliance and mid-project checks.

05

Daily reporting & construction support

Regular aerial checks that cut down on site visits and give project teams a clearer view of progress.

06

Aerial spraying

Spraying by drone, applied from the air with no ground equipment on the field or slope.

07

Agricultural services

Crop seeding, fertilizer application and growth monitoring.

08

Seed pelleting: engineered soil contact

Coming soon. The pelleting process is tailored to site-specific needs using the client’s seed. Pelletized seeds with hydrophilic substrate have increased germination rates in challenging environments. Pelletized seed spreads evenly and accurately with less drift from wind. Read about this service
