Services

What we fly

Commercial payloads, careful planning and crews who know pipeline and construction sites.

01 / Seed & material dispersal

Land reclamation done right

Our GPS-guided drones deliver seed, mulch and granular material to hard-to-reach zones without tearing up the ground or putting your crew on the slope. The EAVision J150 is our baseline aircraft for this work.

Used on: right-of-way seeding in BC, 2026.

Right-of-way seeding · BC

02 / Heavy material lifting

No crane? No problem.

We move seedlings, materials and gear by drone across rivers, steep or roadless terrain, and remote sites, reducing helicopter and ground-crew risk.

Used on: heavy-lift delivery in BC, October 2026.

Coming soon: major heavy-lift upgrades.

Heavy-lift flight · BC

03

Land & pipeline measurement

Measure trench volume and surface contours accurately for pre-job planning, compliance or mid-project verification.

04

Daily reporting & construction support

Regular aerial checks that reduce on-site visits and improve project oversight. We flew construction-support flights on a pipeline spread from August to October 2026.

05

Aerial spraying

Drone spraying for sites and fields where ground equipment is slow, damaging or can’t reach. Our DJI Agras T40 and T70P are built for spraying work.

06

Agricultural services

From crop seeding and fertilizer application to growth monitoring, we offer efficient, lower-impact options for modern agriculture.
